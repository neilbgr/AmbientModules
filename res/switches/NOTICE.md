stock `CKSS_0.svg`/`CKSS_1.svg` (VCV Rack Component Library), rotated 90
degrees (`matrix(0,1,-1,0,20.64111,0)` wrapping the unmodified original
paths) so the slider moves left/right instead of up/down. Used by
LunarMixer's VU/Peak switch.
